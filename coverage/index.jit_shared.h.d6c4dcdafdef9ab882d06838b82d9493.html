68 // The unit of native compilation: a whole function, or one piece of a function too large to
69 // compile at once (see jit_split_function). Pieces split a function at the start of a block (or
70 // inside one too long for a piece), and control moves between them through the driver: a piece
71 // exits to a pc in another piece like a GOTO out of the function, and the driver enters a piece at
