12 // header-only by necessity (templates over two asmjit compiler types), and
13 // is free to have heavy includes. It is one of two shared JIT layers:
14 //
15 // jit_shared.{h,cpp} - the runtime layer: plain data types the whole
16 // engine sees (via jit.h) and the driver .cpp
